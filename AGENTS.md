# AGENTS.md

Follow the repository's detailed agent workflow in [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md) before making changes. That workflow is currently written in Japanese and governs report submissions.

Documentation language policy:
- `README.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md` and `site/README.md` are the English primary documents.
- `README.zh-CN.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.zh-CN.md` and `site/README.zh-CN.md` are Simplified Chinese translations.
- `README.ja.md` and `site/README.ja.md` preserve Japanese documentation.
- For a new report, keep the three report indexes synchronized and do not change existing benchmark results or attachments.
